About Yek

Yek Languages is building practical language-learning tools designed to help people learn languages and connect across cultures.

Our first product, Yek Flashcards, is being developed for both English speakers learning Persian (Farsi) and Persian speakers learning English. The goal is to make vocabulary practice simple, accessible, and useful for real learners, with features such as audio pronunciation, progress tracking, and flexible study sessions.

Yek is starting with Persian and English, but the vision extends beyond a single language pair. We want to build learning experiences that are thoughtful about language, culture, accessibility, and the needs of the people who use them.

Yek Languages is currently in development. We're building, testing, learning from feedback, and improving the experience one step at a time.
